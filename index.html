    About Jam-in!

    Record your voice over any YouTube video and layer your own vocal takes right in the browser.

    • Recording starts as you play; pause will keep or discard the take.
    • Seeking mid-take discards that recording.
    • Wear headphones for the cleanest capture — set Microphone mode in the menu.
    • Takes are stored locally on this device. Export or import them as .jmn files.

    Works offline as an installable app. No account, no upload — your voice stays on your device.
